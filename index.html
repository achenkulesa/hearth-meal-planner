Miso glazed salmon
Brush, broil 10 min, done. Slightly sweet glaze — kids usually love it.
⏱ 15 min total
🔥 Broiler
👨‍👩‍👧 Kids eat it too
White miso + mirin + soy + pinch of sugar. Brush over salmon, let sit 10 min. Broil 8–10 min until caramelized. Serve with rice (you have it) and frozen veg (you have it). Mild and sweet enough for the 5-year-old.
Need to buy:
Salmon fillets (4) White miso paste Mirin
✦ New recipe
Korean braised short ribs
15 min prep, Instant Pot does the rest. Soy + garlic + Asian pear — slightly sweet, deeply savory.
⏱ 15 min prep + 40 min IP
🍲 Instant Pot
👨‍👩‍👧 Kids-friendly sweetness
Blend: soy sauce, garlic, ginger, Asian pear or apple (you have them), sesame oil, brown sugar. Pour over short ribs, pressure cook 40 min. Fall-off-the-bone. Serve over rice with scallions. Great batch cook — leftovers reheat perfectly for the week.
Need to buy:
Beef short ribs (2–3 lbs) Fresh ginger Sesame oil Scallions
